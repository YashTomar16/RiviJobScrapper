# Problem Statement

## Context

Riviera Partners tracks hiring activity across a target list of 200–300 portfolio companies and technology organizations to identify executive search opportunities.

Today, this process is largely manual. Researchers regularly visit each company's careers page, review all open positions, and identify roles relevant to the firm's search practice. This approach is:

- Time-consuming
- Difficult to scale
- Prone to missed opportunities as job postings are added, updated, or removed frequently

## Objective

Automate the monitoring of these career pages and maintain an up-to-date repository of relevant openings.

The system should:

1. Continuously scan the careers pages of the predefined companies
2. Extract new and updated vacancies
3. Filter openings to include only relevant functions and seniority levels

### In-scope functions

- Technology
- Product
- Engineering
- Data
- AI
- Machine Learning

### In-scope seniority levels

Positions across all seniority levels, including:

- Individual Contributors (ICs)
- Manager
- Senior Manager
- Head
- Director
- Senior Director
- Vice President
- SVP
- CTO
- Chief AI Officer
- Chief Product Officer
- Other C-level executives

### Out-of-scope functions

Exclude non-relevant functions such as:

- Sales
- Marketing
- Finance
- HR
- Legal
- Operations

## Desired Outcome

A centralized, automatically updated database of relevant technology leadership and specialist hiring activity that enables Riviera Partners' research and sales enablement teams to:

- Identify client opportunities faster
- Prioritize **Top Targets** (known clients) using past search/placement context alongside new openings
- Reduce manual effort
- Ensure no strategic hiring signals are missed

See `Docs/top-targets.md` for the Top Targets cohort plan.
